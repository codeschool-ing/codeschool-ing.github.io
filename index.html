Frequently asked questions

What we hear the most — and, flagged, what is still being decided.

Do I need a computer at home?

Yes — the courses are hands-on and you follow along by doing. A simple computer with internet and a browser is all you need.

Are classes live or recorded?

Recorded, all of them. You watch whenever you can, as many times as you need, and the exercises correct themselves as you go. There are no live sessions and no fixed timetable — the pace is entirely yours.

Do I have to take the whole track?

No. A track is a recommended order — if you only need one course from it, watch just that one.

Is the certificate recognised?

We issue a continuing-education certificate with the workload and a validation code, accepted as proof of professional qualification.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, on your own account screen, without asking anyone. Access lasts until the end of the period you already paid for, and nothing is charged after that. Certificates you already earned stay valid forever — they record something you did, and losing access does not undo it.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Credit card and Pix. [to confirm with the payment provider before charging starts]

Can I buy a single course without subscribing?

No. What is sold is access to the whole school, because the point here is the track — the order of the courses and what each one unlocks — and a course bought on its own loses exactly that. The first course of every track is free, so you can try before paying anything.

Can my company subscribe for the team?

Not yet as a company plan: today each person subscribes their own account. A plan for teams, with invoicing by company registration, will be built when there is a company asking for it — write to us if that is your case.

Everything is self-service

Create your account, pick a track, study, sit the exams and issue your certificate — on your own, whenever you want. Changing your e-mail, your password or your name, exporting your data and deleting your account are all in the account screen.
