AI in Development

Code with company: coding assistants, agents and AI built into your own projects.

Either JavaScript or Python is enough. The course brings the AI part, not the language.

What you will learn

  • How language models work: embeddings, vectors and RAG
  • Coding assistants in the editor: review, refactoring and documentation

  • Providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) with cost and safety under control
