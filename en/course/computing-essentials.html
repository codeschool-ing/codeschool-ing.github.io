Course

Essential Computing

The start of everything: understanding the computer from the inside, mastering everyday tools and losing the fear of touching things.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: beginner

What you need first

None. This is the school’s first course.

What you will learn

  • Hardware components and what each one does
  • Connection types: cable, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC
  • Windows, files, folders and organisation
  • Office suites: documents, spreadsheets and presentations
  • Personal cloud and collaborative work
  • Home networking basics and first diagnostics

Full topic list

  • Processor, RAM, storage and motherboard
  • Graphics card, power supply, cooling and case
  • Peripherals: monitor, keyboard, mouse, printer and scanner
  • Ports and connectors: USB, HDMI, DisplayPort, Ethernet and audio
  • Wireless connections: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC and infrared
  • Assembling, powering up and setting up a computer from scratch
  • The filesystem: folders, extensions, shortcuts and search
  • Personal backup and organising documents
  • Microsoft Office suite: Word, Excel and PowerPoint
  • Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets and Slides
  • iCloud, OneDrive and Google Drive: syncing and sharing
  • Spreadsheets in practice: formulas, filters and charts
  • Home networking basics: modem, router and the Wi-Fi password
  • System-independent diagnosis: isolate, test and swap
  • Signs of a hardware problem and when to stop pushing
  • Using it sensibly: passwords, updates and common scams
