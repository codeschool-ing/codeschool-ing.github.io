Front-end with Angular

The framework of the bank, the insurer and the government: opinionated, complete, and TypeScript from the first line.

  • 90 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Both are required. TypeScript is taught here from scratch: Angular assumes it, so this course does not.

What you will learn

  • Components, templates and signal-based reactivity
  • Dependency injection: the idea the whole framework is built around
  • Reactive forms, typed, with validation that scales
  • Router, guards, lazy loading and standalone components
  • HttpClient, interceptors and RxJS where it actually earns its place
  • State with signals and services, and an overview of NgRx

Full topic list

  • Why Angular is a framework where React is a library, and what that buys
  • The Angular CLI: generating, building, testing and updating a version
  • Standalone components, and the NgModule code you will still meet
  • Templates: interpolation, property binding and event binding
  • Control flow in the template: @if, @for and @switch
  • Signals: state, computed and effect
  • Change detection: zone.js, OnPush and where it is heading without zones
  • Component communication: input, output and content projection
  • The lifecycle hooks, and which of them you actually need
  • Dependency injection: providers, tokens and hierarchical injectors
  • Services, singletons and the scope an instance really has
  • HttpClient: typed requests, interceptors and error handling
  • RxJS: the operators that pay for themselves, and when a signal is enough
  • Reactive forms: typed controls, groups and arrays
  • Custom validators, asynchronous validation and error messages
  • Template-driven forms, and why the reactive ones win as the form grows
  • Router: parameters, child routes, resolvers and redirects
  • Guards, lazy loading and preloading strategies
  • Pipes: built in, pure, impure and your own
  • Directives, structural and attribute
  • Angular Material and the CDK
  • State: services with signals, and an overview of NgRx
  • Server-side rendering and hydration with Angular SSR
  • Testing: TestBed, harnesses and what belongs at each level
  • A look at React, Vue and Svelte, from where Angular stands
