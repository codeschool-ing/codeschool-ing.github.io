AI systems fail in new ways. Learn to attack your own before someone else does — and to defend them.

  • Direct and indirect prompt injection

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications
  • Bias, fairness and content moderation
  • Red teaming: planning, executing and reporting
  • Defences: filters, tool isolation and human review
