NoSQL: Modelling and Operations

The other families of database, and what changes when you have to model and operate one: document, key-value and wide column.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Requires administration: the operational vocabulary transfers, the model does not.

What you will learn

  • When the relational model is the wrong tool — and when it is not
  • MongoDB: documents, indexes, replica set and sharding
  • Redis: structures, persistence, eviction and cluster
  • Cassandra: partition key, tunable consistency and repair
  • Backup and monitoring in each of the three
  • Choosing: the access pattern decides, not the brand
