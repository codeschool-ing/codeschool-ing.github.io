Course

Design Patterns and Principles

The structural vocabulary of the profession: SOLID, DDD, CQRS, TDD and the paradigms that hold design decisions up.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires back-end experience: the patterns are read in code you have already written.

What you will learn

  • Object orientation beyond the syntax
  • SOLID and the principles behind sustainable code
  • Presentation patterns: MVC, MVP and MVVM
  • CQRS, eventual consistency and ACID versus CAP
  • Domain-Driven Design and test-driven development
  • Functional and reactive programming and the actor model

Full topic list

  • Object orientation: encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism and composition
  • Composition over inheritance: why almost always
  • SOLID: single responsibility, open/closed, Liskov substitution
  • SOLID: interface segregation and dependency inversion
  • Dependency injection and inversion of control
  • Creational, structural and behavioural patterns (GoF) in practice
  • MVC, MVP and MVVM: where each one makes sense
  • CQRS: separating reads from writes
  • Event sourcing and eventual consistency
  • ACID and the CAP theorem: what you gain and what you give up
  • Domain-Driven Design: ubiquitous language and bounded contexts
  • Entities, aggregates, value objects and repositories
  • Test-driven development (TDD): the red-green-refactor cycle
  • Refactoring: code smells and what to do about them
  • Functional programming: immutability, pure functions and side effects
  • Reactive programming: streams, observables and backpressure
  • The actor model: isolated state and message passing
  • Concurrency and parallelism: where the patterns break down
  • Choosing the pattern for the problem, not for your CV
