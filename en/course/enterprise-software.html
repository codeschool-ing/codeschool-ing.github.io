Enterprise Software and Integration

The reality of large companies: ERP, CRM, BPM and the work of integrating systems nobody is allowed to switch off.

  • 50 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

No experience with ERP or CRM assumed — that is what the course is for. It does assume you have designed a system before.

What you will learn

  • The enterprise ecosystem: ERP, CRM, BPM and document management
  • SAP, Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics
  • Process automation with BPM and BPEL
  • Enterprise service bus (ESB) and legacy integration
  • Migration and coexistence strategies

Full topic list

  • What an ERP is and why it constrains the architecture
  • SAP: ERP, HANA and Business Objects
  • Microsoft Dynamics and the Microsoft ecosystem inside the company
  • Salesforce: CRM, platform and customisation
  • Document management (DMS) and the EMC/OpenText case
  • IBM BPM and process automation tools
  • BPM and BPEL: modelling and executing business processes
  • Enterprise service bus (ESB) and SOAP in legacy environments
  • Integration by file, by database, by API and by event
  • Queues and messaging in enterprise integration
  • Enterprise integration patterns (EIP)
  • Systems that cannot stop: windows, contracts and SLAs
  • Strangling the legacy (strangler fig) and coexistence
  • Data migration: quality, reconciliation and rollback
  • Buy versus build: how to justify the decision
