Course

Networks and Protocols

How data travels — and what to do when it stops travelling.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Requires the Linux command line: every diagnosis here is run from a terminal.

What you will learn

  • The OSI model and the TCP/IP stack
  • DNS, HTTP, HTTPS and SSL/TLS in practice
  • SSH: remote access, keys and tunnels
  • File transfer: FTP and SFTP
  • Email protocols: SMTP, IMAP, POP3S, SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • Network diagnostics: dig, traceroute, tcpdump

Full topic list

  • The OSI model: the seven layers and what lives in each one
  • The TCP/IP model and how it maps onto OSI
  • TCP and UDP: ports, handshake and when to use each
  • DNS: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX and TXT records and recursive resolution
  • HTTP, HTTPS and the TLS handshake
  • SSL/TLS: certificates, certificate authorities and validity
  • SSH: keys, agents, port forwarding and tunnels
  • File transfer: FTP, SFTP and SCP
  • Email: SMTP, IMAP, POP3S, SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • Diagnostics: ping, dig, nslookup, traceroute, mtr, netstat/ss and tcpdump
