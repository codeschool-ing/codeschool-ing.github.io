Course

Analytical Techniques: Time Series, A/B Tests and Cohorts

The three analyses companies ask for most: where are we heading, which version is better and why customers disappear.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Requires statistics. Visualisation helps but is not assumed — these are the three analyses companies ask for most.

What you will learn

  • Time series: trend, seasonality and noise
  • Forecasting and evaluating error
  • A/B testing: design, sample size and reading the result
  • Cohort analysis and retention
  • Machine learning applied to BI
  • Recognising algorithmic bias

Full topic list

  • Components of a time series: trend, seasonality, cycle and noise
  • Decomposition and moving averages
  • Exponential smoothing and an overview of ARIMA and Prophet
  • Forecasting: horizon, interval and revision
  • Measuring forecast error: MAE, RMSE and MAPE
  • Pitfalls: holiday seasonality, regime change and incomplete data
  • A/B testing: hypothesis, primary metric and success criterion
  • Calculating sample size and test duration
  • Randomisation, contamination and the novelty effect
  • Reading the result: significance, minimum detectable effect and decision
  • Multiple testing and the problem of peeking early
  • Cohort analysis: defining the group and the observation window
  • Retention curves, churn and customer value over time
  • Conversion funnels and where the user gives up
  • Segmentation: RFM and clustering
  • Supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning: an overview
  • Classification and regression applied to business problems
  • Algorithmic bias: where it comes from and how to recognise it
  • Mitigation strategies and ethical use of the model
