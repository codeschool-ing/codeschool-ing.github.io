Streaming and Event Processing

Data that does not wait for the nightly job: events arriving all the time, processed as they arrive — and still correct when one turns up late.

  • 70 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires Python. It also assumes you have built a batch pipeline: streaming is the second way to move data, and almost every decision in it is a reaction to what batch cannot do.

What you will learn

  • Events, the log and topics: the streaming model
  • Apache Kafka: partitions, producers, consumers and consumer groups
  • Delivery guarantees: at-most-once, at-least-once and exactly-once
  • Event time, windows, watermarks and late data
  • Processing the stream: Spark Structured Streaming, Flink and Kafka Streams
  • Change data capture, and operating a stream in production
