Front-end with Vue

The one you can adopt a page at a time: the gentlest curve of the four, with a full-stack answer of its own in Nuxt.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Both are required. It is the easiest of the four to start with, and the only one that adopts an existing page a piece at a time.

What you will learn

  • Single-file components and the Composition API
  • Reactivity: ref, reactive, computed and the traps
  • Props, emits, slots and provide/inject
  • Vue Router and Pinia
  • Nuxt: routing by file, rendering modes and data fetching
  • TypeScript in Vue, and where it costs something
