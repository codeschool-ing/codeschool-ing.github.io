Course

Data Cleaning and Preparation

Eighty per cent of the work is here: dirty data in, wrong decision out. Learn to treat it before you analyse it.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Both are required.

What you will learn

  • Diagnosing data quality
  • Missing, duplicate and inconsistent values
  • Standardising formats, text and categories
  • Outliers: identifying them and deciding
  • Transformation and enrichment
  • Exploratory data analysis (EDA) as a working step

Full topic list

  • Dimensions of data quality: accuracy, completeness, consistency and timeliness
  • Profiling: getting to know the table before touching it
  • Missing values: why they are missing and what that means
  • Strategies for missing values: drop, fill, impute or flag
  • Exact and fuzzy duplicates; record matching
  • Standardising text: case, accents, whitespace and abbreviations
  • Standardising dates, currencies, units and codes
  • Inconsistent categories and grouping labels
  • Outliers: typo, real event or fraud
  • Wrong data types and safe conversions
  • Joining tables: keys, cardinality and orphan records
  • Transformations: normalisation, binning and derived variables
  • Pivoting and unpivoting (wide and long format)
  • Enrichment from external sources
  • Exploratory analysis: distributions, relationships and first hypotheses
  • Tools: Excel, SQL, pandas and dplyr
  • Recording every transformation: reproducibility and auditing
