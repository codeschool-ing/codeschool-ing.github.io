Course

Communication and Storytelling with Data

Analysis nobody understands changes nothing. Learn to tell the story in the data and to be taken seriously in the meeting.

  • 50 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Requires an analysis of your own to tell. It is the least technical course of the BI track.

What you will learn

  • The structure of a story told with data
  • Adapting the message to the audience
  • Presentation and dashboard design
  • The executive summary: one page that decides
  • Business acumen and critical thinking
  • Stakeholder and change management

Full topic list

  • Why data alone does not convince anyone
  • Story structure: context, conflict, evidence and recommendation
  • Starting from the conclusion: the inverted pyramid
  • Adapting to the audience: board, management, technical teams and clients
  • One message per slide, one chart per message
  • Presentation design: contrast, alignment, repetition and whitespace
  • Dashboard design for someone who has thirty seconds
  • The executive summary: one page with finding, impact and next step
  • Writing an actionable recommendation instead of an observation
  • Anticipating the hard question and the objection
  • Business acumen: speaking the company’s language
  • Critical thinking: questioning the source, the sample and the incentive
  • Stakeholder management: expectations, priorities and continuous communication
  • Change management: why a department resists the number
  • Project management basics for analytical deliverables
  • Portfolio: an end-to-end analysis project that shows your process
