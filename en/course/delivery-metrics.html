Delivery and Metrics: Flow, DORA and Incidents

Answering "when will it be ready" and "how are we doing" with something other than an opinion: flow, the four DORA metrics, incidents, and the on-call rota somebody has to carry.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires process and estimation. It assumes you have shipped software in a team with a pipeline and production — it does not teach deployment, it teaches how to measure and decide.

What you will learn

  • Flow: work in progress, queues and cycle time
  • The four DORA metrics, and how they get gamed
  • Forecasting without pretending to predict
  • Incidents: severity, command and the postmortem
  • On-call: rota, alert fatigue and the cost of the pager
  • Reporting upwards: what a dashboard proves and what it does not
