Course

Systems Architecture

The decisions that determine whether a system grows or seizes up — the shape, the data across it, the patterns that keep it standing when a part fails, and how to communicate all three.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires Docker and back-end experience: this course decides between designs, it does not teach a language.

What you will learn

  • Monolith, microservices, SOA, serverless and service mesh
  • The twelve factors of modern applications
  • Data across services: CAP, consistency, replication and sharding
  • Asynchronous messaging with Kafka and RabbitMQ
  • Resilience patterns: circuit breaker, retry, bulkhead and throttling
  • Cloud design patterns: CQRS, event sourcing, saga and the strangler fig
  • Search, real time, and documenting the decisions you made
