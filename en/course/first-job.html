Course

From Study to Hired: CV, Interviews and the First 90 Days

The part that comes after the last course: a CV that gets read, a profile that gets found, an interview that holds no surprises, and the first ninety days nobody explains.

  • 50 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

No prerequisite beyond having something to show, which the portfolio project produces. The course is the same whichever track you came from — the market changes less often than the technology does.

What you will learn

  • The market you are entering: roles, seniority and what is really hired
  • A CV that survives ten seconds, and a profile that gets found
  • Applying: where, how many, and how to follow up
  • The technical interview: live coding, take-home and the questions behind them
  • Talking about money without guessing
  • The first ninety days, and the probation nobody explains
