Data Pipelines and ETL

The backbone of the profession: moving and transforming data reliably and repeatably — and proving it still works after somebody changed the source.

  • 70 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Requires modelling and SQL, plus Python for the orchestrator.

What you will learn

  • Ingestion types: batch, real time and hybrid
  • ETL and ELT: extract, transform and load
  • Orchestration with Apache Airflow: DAGs, scheduling, sensors and backfill
  • Version-controlled transformations with dbt: models, tests and lineage
  • An overview of Luigi, Prefect and Dagster
  • Idempotency, reprocessing, testing and failure handling
