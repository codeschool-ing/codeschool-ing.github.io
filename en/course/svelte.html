Front-end with Svelte

The one that compiles itself away: no virtual DOM, almost no runtime shipped, and reactivity that reads like the language it is written in.

  • 70 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Both are required. The smallest of the four to learn and the smallest to ship — the compiler does what the others do at runtime.

What you will learn

  • Runes: the reactivity that replaced the magic
  • Components, props, snippets and events
  • SvelteKit: routing, load functions and form actions
  • Rendering: SSR, prerendering and the adapter you deploy with
  • Stores and context: state that outlives a component
  • TypeScript, testing, and what the compiler gives you for nothing
