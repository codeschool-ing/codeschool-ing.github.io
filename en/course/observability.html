Observability and Operations

Seeing what is happening in production — and acting before the user notices. Starting with instrumenting the service yourself, because nothing is visible that was not made visible.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires something already running to observe. The pipeline that deploys the agent is not assumed.

What you will learn

  • The three pillars, and instrumenting your own service with OpenTelemetry
  • Metrics with Prometheus, dashboards with Grafana, and what they cost
  • Centralised, structured logging: Elastic Stack, Loki, Graylog
  • Distributed tracing: spans, context propagation and sampling
  • SLI, SLO, error budget and an alert worth waking up for
  • The incident: on-call, severity, communication and the blameless postmortem
  • Health checks and service mesh: Istio, Linkerd and Envoy
