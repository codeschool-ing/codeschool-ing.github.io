Kubernetes: Orchestration in Production

One container is easy; hundreds of them, with zero-downtime updates and automatic scaling, is Kubernetes.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires Docker. This is the most advanced infrastructure course in the catalogue. GitOps, observability and pipelines appear as an overview — each has its own course after this one.

What you will learn

  • Cluster architecture and your first application online
  • Deployments, services, Ingress and pod-to-pod networking
  • Configuration, secrets and persistent storage with CSI
  • Resources, scheduling, probes and autoscaling of pods and nodes
  • Cluster security: RBAC, network policies and pod security
  • Helm, release patterns, extending with CRDs and day-to-day operations
