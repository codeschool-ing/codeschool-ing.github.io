Backup, Replication and High Availability

What stands between an incident and a resignation letter: a backup that restores, a replica that takes over and a plan that was rehearsed.

  • 70 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires administration. This is the course that separates a database that survives from one that has been lucky.

What you will learn

  • Full, incremental and continuous backup, and where each one fails
  • Point-in-time recovery, and the restore drill almost nobody runs
  • RPO and RTO: agreeing the number before the incident, not during it
  • Streaming and logical replication, synchronous or not
  • Failover, split-brain and connection pooling
  • Disaster recovery and a runbook that outlives the person who wrote it
