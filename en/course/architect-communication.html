Course

Communication, Consulting and Technical Leadership

The part that sinks more architects than any technical difficulty: explaining, persuading, growing people and saying no.

  • 50 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

Requires the management course. It is the least technical course in the catalogue, and the one architects fail on most.

What you will learn

  • Writing to be read: documentation and proposals
  • Presenting technical decisions to non-technical people
  • Internal consulting: diagnose before you prescribe
  • Growing people: mentoring, code review and pairing
  • Negotiating deadline, scope and technical debt
  • Building a technical reputation without becoming a salesperson

Full topic list

  • Writing clearly: structure, precision and economy of words
  • The one-pager, the technical proposal and the internal RFC
  • Adapting the message: board, product, team and client
  • Translating technical risk into business risk
  • Presentations: tell the story of the decision, not the list of technologies
  • Active listening and uncovering the real need
  • Internal consulting: diagnose before proposing a solution
  • Saying no with an alternative: the art of refusing well
  • Mediating technical conflict between people and between teams
  • Mentoring and development plans for developers
  • Code review as a teaching tool
  • Pair and mob programming
  • Negotiating deadline, scope, quality and technical debt
  • Communicating an incident: during, after and to whom
  • Blameless postmortems and a culture of learning
  • Technical reputation: talks, articles and community
