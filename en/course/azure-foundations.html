Azure: Foundations, Identity and Network

The ground floor of Azure: how the tenant is divided, who is allowed to do what, what it costs and how the packets move.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: intermediate

What you need first

Cloud and Serverless, or the equivalent: knowing what IaaS, IAM and a virtual network are.

What you will learn

  • Tenant, management groups, subscriptions and resource groups
  • Microsoft Entra ID: users, groups, service principals and managed identities
  • RBAC and Azure Policy: permission and guardrail are two different things
  • Cost Management: budgets, tags and reading a bill that arrived wrong
  • VNet, network security groups, Private Link and the load balancers
  • Azure CLI, Cloud Shell and the Resource Manager underneath everything
