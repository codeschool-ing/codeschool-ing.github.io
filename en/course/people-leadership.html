Leading People: One-to-Ones, Feedback and Hiring

The half of the job no promotion prepares anyone for. One-to-ones that are not status meetings, feedback that changes something, hiring without theatre, and the conversations nobody wants to have.

  • 60 hours
  • Level: advanced

What you need first

No technical prerequisite. It assumes you have worked in a team and watched a project go wrong — the subject is people, and the examples come from software.

What you will learn

  • The first ninety days: from doing the work to answering for it
  • One-to-ones: a meeting that is not a status report
  • Feedback that changes behaviour, and the kind that only relieves you
  • Growth: levels, expectations and the promotion conversation
  • Hiring: interview, calibration and the cost of getting it wrong
  • Difficult conversations: performance, conflict and letting someone go

  • Feedback: situation, behaviour, impact — and asking before telling
  • Positive feedback as calibration, not as decoration
  • When feedback stops being feedback and becomes a performance conversation
  • Career levels: what "senior" means once it is written down
  • Expectations, evidence and building a promotion case that survives a panel
  • Growth without promotion: scope, depth and work that is visible
  • Hiring: the job description, the funnel and the time it really takes
  • Structured interviews, and the questions that predict nothing
  • Take-home, pairing or live coding: what each one actually measures
  • Calibration between interviewers, and the bias that survives good intentions
  • Onboarding: the first week, the first commit and the first on-call shift
  • Underperformance: diagnosing it before deciding anything
  • The improvement plan, written honestly

  • Managing up: your own manager, your peers and the promise you cannot make
