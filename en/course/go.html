Course

Go

The language Docker, Kubernetes and Terraform chose: little syntax to memorise, a lot of software to build.

  • 80 hours
  • Level: beginner

What you need first

None. This course teaches the language from scratch — it is the entry point for anyone who has never written Go.

What you will learn

  • Syntax, types, constants and conversions
  • Composite types: arrays, slices, maps and structs
  • Functions, closures and pointers without arithmetic
  • Methods, implicit interfaces and composition
  • Generics: generic functions and types with constraints
  • Errors as values, modules and project organisation
